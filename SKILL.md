## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one private diary page drawn in ordinary blue ballpoint on cheap lined notebook paper. The printed lines must run through the drawing and not stop for the subject. Build tone with hatching that follows the structure, irregular crossing, repeated strokes and dense marks. No digital grey and no smooth shade. Some passages are intensely observed; neighbouring passages stop suddenly into sparse contour, searching lines and accidental scratches. The subject may be off-centre, cropped or partly absent. Real hand pressure, hesitation and corrections stay visible. Any words are written in the same ballpoint, like notes made while looking. It is not a finished illustration, a full background, a photo trace, a clean vector, a mechanical technical drawing or a commercial sketch.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-243/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
